# Buff Cat: Your Friendly Crypto Locking Platform

## What is Buff Cat?

Buff Cat is a decentralized finance (DeFi) platform that lets anyone lock up their cryptocurrency tokens and earn rewards from other users' locked tokens. Think of it like a digital piggy bank that pays you for keeping your money in one place.

## How Does It Work?

### Locking Tokens

- You can lock any type of cryptocurrency for up to 3,000 days
- Minimum lock time is 24 hours
- You can choose between two types of locks:
  1. Flexible Lock: You can unlock anytime
  2. Fixed Lock: You commit to keeping your tokens locked for a specific period

### Earning Rewards

- Every day, 10% of the total pool is distributed to users
- Your rewards depend on:
  - How much you've locked
  - How long you've locked your tokens
  - How many different types of tokens you've locked

### Bonus Opportunities

- Lock multiple different tokens and get extra reward boosts
- Longer lock periods give you more rewards
- Referral program gives both you and your friend a small reward boost

### Flexible and Fair

- Daily reward claims are capped
- You can store rewards for later claim when your locked token perform 10x, 100x etc
- Special rules for stablecoins to keep things balanced

## Key Benefits

- Earn passive income from your crypto
- Flexible locking options
- Rewards for long-term commitment
- Multi-chain support (Base, Ethereum, Solana, and more)

## Important Notes

- There are small fees for locking tokens, unlocking tokens and claiming rewards.
- The platform has built-in security measures.
- Rewards can change based on token activity.

Buff Cat aims to make crypto earning simple, rewarding, and accessible to everyone!
